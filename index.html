The kitchen

Bringing tradition & modern taste together

Narwal Urban Village offers a dining experience inspired by authentic Indian flavours blended with modern culinary creativity. The restaurant is designed for families, friends, and food lovers who appreciate rich taste, premium ingredients, and genuine hospitality.

From flavourful appetizers to signature main courses and handcrafted desserts, every dish is prepared with passion and freshness — the sort of cooking that makes an ordinary Tuesday memorable.

How a dish gets to you

Four hands, one plate

Step one

The marinade

Yogurt, ginger, garlic and whole spices ground the same morning. Chicken and lamb sit in it overnight — never less.

Step two

The tandoor

A clay wall at 480°C. Skewers go in vertically so the fat renders down through the meat instead of pooling under it.

Step three

The gravy

Onions cooked down slowly until they collapse. Tomatoes, cream, cashew paste. No shortcuts, no powder bases.

Step four

The pass

Plated, checked, sent. Bread comes out last so it reaches the table still too hot to hold comfortably.

What we won't change

Some things are worth being stubborn about

We grind our own masala. We make paneer in house. We keep the vegetarian menu as long as the meat one — sixteen dishes, not an afterthought section at the back.

And we still write (N) next to anything with nuts, (GF) for gluten free and (V) for vegan, because guessing is not hospitality.
